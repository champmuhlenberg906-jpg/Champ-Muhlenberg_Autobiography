Chapter 2: My Journey from Liberia to Rwanda

A New Chapter in My Life

After graduating from Bishop Marvin Noah High School in Liberia, a new opportunity came into my life. I was given an opportunity from Oregon to travel to Rwanda to continue my education.

Leaving Liberia for Rwanda was an important turning point in my life. It meant stepping into a new environment, meeting new people, experiencing a different culture, and preparing myself for a new academic journey.

Adjusting to a New Environment

Moving from Liberia to Rwanda was not just a change of location; it was the beginning of a new chapter filled with opportunities, challenges, and lessons.

As I settled into my new environment, I understood that success would require determination, patience, discipline, and hard work.

I knew that coming to Rwanda was an opportunity I needed to take seriously. My goal was to make good use of the opportunity, continue my education, and build a better future.

My Determination

My journey has taught me that opportunities should never be taken for granted. Every challenge is an opportunity to learn, grow, and become stronger.

I remain grateful for the opportunity to continue my education in Rwanda. I am determined to keep working toward my dreams and to make a positive difference in the lives of others.

My journey from Liberia to Rwanda is more than a change of country; it is a story of hope, courage, education, and determination.
