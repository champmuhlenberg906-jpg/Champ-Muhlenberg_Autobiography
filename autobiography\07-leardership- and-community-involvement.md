Chapter 7: Leadership and Community Involvement

Developing Leadership Skills

Leadership is an important part of personal growth. I believe that leadership is not only about holding a position but also about serving others, accepting responsibilities, and making positive contributions.

During my university journey at the University of Lay Adventists of Kigali (UNILAK), I had the opportunity to serve as Assistant Sports and Recreation Coordinator within the student leadership team.

This responsibility has given me an opportunity to contribute to student activities, sports, recreation, teamwork, and campus engagement.

I believe that participating in leadership helps develop communication, teamwork, discipline, responsibility, and decision-making skills.

My ambition is to continue growing as a leader who encourages others, supports teamwork, and contributes positively to the community.

I want my leadership journey to reflect service, commitment, integrity, and a willingness to learn.
